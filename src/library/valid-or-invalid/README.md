# "Valid or invalid?" logic game (saved for later)

Six arguments students judge one at a time: the Socrates syllogism, two "affirming the consequent"
traps, a valid-but-false-premise robot cat, a "no fish fly" form and a some/some trick. Each reveal
shows the argument's form and a one-line explanation; a "common trap" card unlocks when they meet it.

Taken out of AI History Part 1 to keep that deck on history. Good for a logic or critical-thinking
lesson, or as a warm-up.

To use it in a deck:
1. paste `slide.html` into the deck's shell where the slide should go
2. add `slide.css` inside the deck's `<style>`
3. paste `slide.js` into the deck's app.js next to the other slide modules
4. it relies on the shared helpers and styles every deck has: `$`, `A`, `.syl`, `.term`/`.tA`/`.tB`/`.tC`,
   `.form`, `.flip`, `.tag`, `.btn`, `.b-yes`/`.b-no`, `.callout`, `.card`, `.two`, `.stack`, `.rise`
